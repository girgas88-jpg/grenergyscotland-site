Unvented hot water cylinders

Supply, installation, servicing and repairs. G3 qualified.

An unvented cylinder runs your hot water straight off the mains. Proper shower, no tank in the loft. Before I quote I check your mains flow, the space and where the discharge pipe can go, so you get one that suits the house.

All work is properly tested, commissioned and installed to local building regulations and manufacturer's instructions, using quality equipment and materials throughout. You get the paperwork for the manufacturer's warranty.

Questions

What is an unvented cylinder?

A sealed hot water cylinder fed straight off the mains, so your taps and shower run at mains pressure. No cold tank in the loft.

Why does an unvented cylinder need a special qualification?

It stores hot water under pressure, so by law you need G3 to install or work on one. I've got it.
