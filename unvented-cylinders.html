Cylinder questions

What is an unvented cylinder?

A sealed hot water cylinder fed straight off the incoming mains, so your hot taps and shower run at mains pressure. No cold tank in the loft.

Why does it need a special qualification?

Because it stores hot water under pressure. Installing or working on one legally requires the G3 qualification, which I hold.

Will it work in my house?

It depends mainly on your incoming mains flow and the space you have. I check both before recommending anything.

Do unvented cylinders need servicing?

Yes, once a year.
