Heat pump questions

Who installs the heat pump?

For new installations I work with an MCS-accredited partner. They design the system and supply the equipment; I install it and commission it as your engineer. You deal with me throughout, from the first visit to handover.

Will a heat pump work in my house?

Many houses suit one; some need insulation or radiator upgrades first. That gets checked as part of the design before you commit.

Is there grant funding available?

Scotland has its own funding, which changes from time to time. Check the current Home Energy Scotland position before you commit. Grant-funded installations generally require MCS certification.
