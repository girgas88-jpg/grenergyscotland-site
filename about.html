About GR Energy Scotland

A one-man heating and gas engineering business based in Torrance, just north of Glasgow.

Who you're dealing with

I'm Gavin, and GR Energy Scotland is me. There's no office, no call centre and no salesman who turns up with an iPad and a discount that expires at midnight. The person who quotes your job is the person who does it, and the person you ring if something isn't right afterwards.

That has an obvious limitation — I can only be in one place at a time, so I won't always be able to get to you the same day. What you get in exchange is continuity: I know what's in your house because I put it there.

Qualifications

Gas Safe registered, number 962889. You can check that on the Gas Safe Register website, and you should — for me and for anyone else you let near your gas.

I also hold the G3 qualification for unvented hot water storage, which is a legal requirement for installing or working on mains-pressure cylinders, and I work on air source heat pumps as well as gas.

How I price

Replacement work is surveyed before it's quoted. That takes longer up front and it's the only way the number stays the same at the end. Quotes are itemised, with the exclusions written down, so you can see what's in and what isn't.

I'm a sole trader and not VAT registered, so there's no 20% added at the end. On a boiler or cylinder replacement that's a real difference against a larger firm, and it's the honest reason a one-man business can often come in lower on the same specification.

Working in Scotland

Building standards work differently here than in England and Wales, and it matters for hot water and heating work. Gas work is notified as required, and unvented installations are commissioned, tested and certified with the paperwork handed over.
