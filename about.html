About GR Energy Scotland

A heating and gas engineering business based in Glasgow.

Who you're dealing with

I'm Gavin. You get the same engineer from start to finish — the person who quotes your job is the person who does it.

My diary is usually full, so book in advance to avoid disappointment. I don't offer 24-hour emergency cover.

Qualifications

Gas Safe registered, number 962889. You can check that on the Gas Safe Register website.

How I price

Replacement work is surveyed before it's quoted, and the quote is a fixed figure in writing.
