Boiler repair, servicing and replacement

Gas Safe registered boiler work across Glasgow and East Dunbartonshire — breakdowns diagnosed properly, replacements surveyed before they're quoted.

Boiler repair

Most breakdowns come down to a handful of things: a failed diverter valve, a blocked condensate, a knackered expansion vessel, a dead pump or a sensor that's drifted out of range. The skill is in telling which, rather than replacing parts until it stops complaining.

I diagnose on site before ordering anything, and I'll tell you what the part costs and what the labour costs separately, so you can see what you're paying for.

Common symptoms I get called out to:

  • Boiler locks out and needs resetting repeatedly
  • Hot water but no heating, or heating but no hot water
  • Pressure dropping and needing topping up every few days
  • Kettling, banging or gurgling from the boiler
  • Radiators cold at the bottom or cold at the far end of the house
  • Boiler firing then cutting out after a couple of minutes

Annual servicing

A proper service is not a wipe-down and a sticker. Casing off, combustion analysed and the readings recorded, seals and electrodes checked, condensate and flue inspected, gas rate checked against the data plate, safety devices tested.

Two reasons to bother: nearly every manufacturer warranty is void without an annual service, and the failures that leave people without heating in the middle of winter are almost always visible months earlier.

Boiler replacement

Every replacement gets surveyed before I quote. I check the gas supply can carry the appliance, the flue route works, the condensate can actually get to a drain, and the existing system isn't so full of sludge that it'll wreck a new heat exchanger.

The quote comes as one figure with the exclusions written down.

A word on system conversions. Swapping a regular boiler and cylinder for a combi is not automatically an upgrade. If you've two bathrooms and a poor incoming mains flow, a combi will disappoint you. I'll measure the flow rate before recommending it either way.

Before you call

Handy to have ready, though don't worry if you haven't:

  • Boiler make and model — it's on the front or inside the case
  • Any fault code on the display
  • System pressure from the gauge
  • Roughly how old the boiler is

Boiler questions

My boiler is showing a fault code — is that fixable?

Usually, yes. A fault code is the boiler telling you which sensor or component it's unhappy with, not a death sentence. Send me the make, model and code and I'll tell you what it points at before I come out.

Should I repair or replace?

Rough rule: if the repair is heading past a third of the cost of a new boiler and the appliance is over about 12 years old, replacement usually wins on running cost and reliability. Under that, repair. I'll give you the figures for both rather than steering you at the expensive one.

Does a service actually do anything?

It keeps most manufacturer warranties valid, which alone pays for itself, and it catches the things that strand people mid-winter — blocked condensate, failing expansion vessels, corroded heat exchangers. It also confirms the appliance is burning safely.

How long does a boiler swap take?

A straight like-for-like combi swap is usually a day. Moving the boiler, changing fuel or converting from a regular system to a combi is typically two days, sometimes more if the gas supply needs upgrading.
