Boilers

Repairs, annual servicing and replacements on combi, system and regular boilers.

Repairs

Repairs on combi, system and regular boilers. I diagnose on site before ordering parts.

Annual servicing

Every service checks the flue, ventilation, heat input, working pressure and safety devices. I'll also check the boiler's efficiency and give you advice on keeping running costs down. Most manufacturer warranties need a service every year.

Replacement

Every replacement is surveyed before I quote, and the quote comes as one fixed figure in writing. All work is properly tested, commissioned and installed to local building regulations and manufacturer's instructions, using quality equipment and materials throughout.

Before you get in touch

Handy to have ready, though don't worry if you haven't:

  • Boiler make and model — it's on the front or inside the case
  • Any fault code on the display
  • System pressure from the gauge
  • Roughly how old the boiler is

Questions

My boiler is showing a fault code — is that fixable?

Usually, yes. Send me the make, model and fault code when you get in touch.

Should I repair or replace?

It depends on the age of the boiler and the cost of the repair. I'll give you the figures for both and let you decide.

Do I need an annual service?

Most manufacturer warranties require one, and it confirms the boiler is working safely and efficiently.

How long does a boiler swap take?

A like-for-like combi swap is usually a day. Moving the boiler or converting the system typically takes two.

Planning a heating project?

Tell me what's up and I'll come back to you with a straight answer. The same engineer from start to finish.
