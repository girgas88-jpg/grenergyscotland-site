Common questions

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes, number 962889. Check any engineer at gassaferegister.co.uk before they touch your gas, and ask to see their card.

How soon can you come out?

My diary's usually booked up in advance, so get in touch early. I don't do 24-hour emergency cover. If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

Do you charge for a quote?

No, quotes for replacement work are free. I survey first and give you a fixed figure in writing, so the price doesn't move once I'm on site.

Can you work on unvented hot water cylinders?

Yes. I'm G3 qualified, which you need to install, service or repair one.

Do you install heat pumps as well as gas boilers?

Yes. For new heat pump installations I work with an MCS-accredited partner. We carry out the system design and order the equipment and materials the job needs; I install and commission it as your engineer, and you deal with me from the first visit to handover.

Planning a heating project?

Tell me what's going on and I'll come back to you with a straight answer.
