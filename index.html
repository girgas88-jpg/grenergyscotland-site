Common questions

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes — Gas Safe register number 962889. You can check any engineer's registration at gassaferegister.co.uk before letting them touch your gas. Always ask to see the card.

How soon can you come out?

My diary is usually booked up in advance, so get in touch early to avoid disappointment. I don't offer 24-hour emergency cover. If you smell gas, call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes for replacement work are free, and I'll survey the property before putting a figure in writing so the price doesn't move once I'm on site.

Can you work on unvented hot water cylinders?

Yes — I hold the G3 qualification required to install, service and repair unvented cylinders.

Do you install heat pumps as well as gas boilers?

Yes. For new heat pump installations I work with an MCS-accredited partner. We carry out the system design and order the equipment and materials the job needs; I install and commission it as your engineer, and you deal with me from the first visit to handover.

Planning a heating project?

Tell me what's up and I'll come back to you with a straight answer. The same engineer from start to finish.
