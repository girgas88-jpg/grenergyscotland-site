Common questions

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes — Gas Safe register number 962889. You can check any engineer's registration at gassaferegister.co.uk before letting them touch your gas. Always ask to see the card.

Do you add VAT to your prices?

No. I'm a sole trader and not VAT registered, so the price I quote is the price you pay — there's no 20% added at the end. On a boiler swap that's a meaningful difference against a VAT-registered firm.

How quickly can you get out to a breakdown?

It depends what's already booked in that week. Get in touch with what the boiler is doing and I'll tell you honestly when I can be there rather than promising same-day and letting you down.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes for replacement work are free, and I'll survey the property before putting a figure in writing so the price doesn't move once I'm on site.

Can you work on unvented hot water cylinders?

Yes — I hold the G3 qualification required to install, commission and sign off unvented cylinders. It's a legal requirement for that work and not every heating engineer holds it.

Do you install heat pumps as well as gas boilers?

Yes. I work across both, which means when you ask whether a heat pump makes sense for your house you get a straight answer rather than a sales pitch — sometimes the honest answer is that your property isn't ready for one yet.

Need a boiler looked at?

Tell me what's up and I'll come back to you with a straight answer and a fixed price. No call-centre, no sales visit — you deal with me directly.
