Annual servicing

Every service checks the flue, ventilation, heat input, working pressure and safety devices. I'll also check the boiler's efficiency and give you advice on keeping running costs down. Most manufacturer warranties need a service every year.

Replacement

Questions

My boiler is showing a fault code — is that fixable?

Usually, yes. Send me the make, model and fault code when you get in touch.

Should I repair or replace?

It depends on the age of the boiler and the cost of the repair. I'll give you the figures for both and let you decide.

Do I need an annual service?

Most manufacturer warranties require one, and it confirms the boiler is working safely and efficiently.

How long does a boiler swap take?

A like-for-like combi swap is usually a day. Moving the boiler or converting the system typically takes two.

What is an unvented cylinder?

A sealed hot water cylinder fed straight off the incoming mains, so your hot taps and shower run at mains pressure. No cold tank in the loft.

Why does an unvented cylinder need a special qualification?

Because it stores hot water under pressure. Installing or working on one legally requires the G3 qualification, which I hold.

Who designs and installs a heat pump?

For new installations I work with an MCS-accredited partner. We carry out the system design and order the equipment and materials the job needs; I install and commission it as your engineer, and you deal with me from the first visit to handover.

Is there grant funding for a heat pump?

Scotland has its own funding, which changes from time to time. Check what is currently available on the Home Energy Scotland website before you commit. Grant-funded installations generally require MCS certification.

Planning a heating project?

Tell me what's up and I'll come back to you with a straight answer. The same engineer from start to finish.
